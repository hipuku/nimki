# Code of conduct

nimki follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews agrees to it.

In short: be considerate, assume good intent, criticise work rather than people, and accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in any form.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer at [hello@hipuku.dev](mailto:hello@hipuku.dev). Reports are read by the maintainer only and handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block an account, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
